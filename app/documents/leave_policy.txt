Company Leave Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-001

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Applies To: All Full-Time Employees

1. Purpose & Objective
This policy outlines the guidelines and procedures for requesting and utilizing employee leave. Our goal is to support work-life balance while ensuring operational continuity across all departments.

2. Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)
Entitlement: All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued on a monthly basis.

Carry Over: A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year, subject to managerial approval. Any additional unused days will expire on March 31st.

Notice Period: Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for leaves longer than 3 days, and 48 hours in advance for single-day requests.

3. Sick Leave & Medical Emergencies
Entitlement: Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Documentation: A medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider is required for any sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days.

Notification: Employees must notify their direct manager and HR via email or messaging within 2 hours of their scheduled shift start time if they are unable to work due to illness.

4. Public & Statutory Holidays
The company observes all officially mandated national and regional public holidays. A complete list of closed office dates for the upcoming year is published on the intranet every December.

5. Parental & Compassionate Leave
Maternity/Paternity Leave: Eligible employees receive paid parental leave in compliance with local statutory regulations. Detailed breakdown sheets can be obtained directly from the HR department.

Bereavement Leave: Up to 3 to 5 days of paid leave are granted in the event of the passing of an immediate family member.

6. General Guidelines & Approval
Manager Approval: Manager approval is mandatory before any planned leave is officially confirmed.

Operational Needs: While every effort is made to accommodate preferred dates, leave requests may be denied or adjusted during peak business cycles or critical project deadlines.

Unauthorised Absence: Taking unapproved leave or failing to report to work without notification for more than 3 consecutive days may result in disciplinary action.
