Company IT Security Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-003

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Applies To: All Employees, Contractors, and Third-Party Vendors

1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines the security standards required to protect company data, networks, and hardware assets against unauthorized access, theft, and cyber threats.

2. Password & Authentication Security
Password Standards: All company accounts require strong passwords consisting of a minimum of 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be updated every 90 days.

Credential Sharing: Employees must never share their passwords, PINs, or security tokens with anyone, including colleagues, managers, or IT support staff.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): MFA must be enabled on all corporate email accounts, cloud storage platforms, and remote VPN connections.

3. Acceptable Use of Hardware & Networks
Company Devices: Laptops, mobile phones, and peripherals provided by the company are for professional use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not violate security guidelines or interfere with job duties.

Secure Networks: When working remotely, employees must connect via a secure corporate VPN and avoid unencrypted public Wi-Fi networks without protection.

Software Installation: Installing unauthorized or unlicensed third-party software, browser extensions, or peer-to-peer file-sharing applications on company devices is strictly prohibited.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality
Access Control: Access to sensitive company information, intellectual property, and personnel records is granted strictly on a "need-to-know" basis according to job responsibilities.

Data Storage: Company data must be stored on approved cloud drives or secure internal servers. Storing sensitive corporate data on unencrypted personal USB drives or personal cloud accounts is prohibited.

5. Incident Reporting
Security Breaches: If you suspect a security incident—such as a phishing email, lost hardware, or unauthorized access—you must report it immediately to the IT Security Team.

Phishing Awareness: Never click on suspicious links or open attachments from unknown senders. When in doubt, verify with IT before acting.

6. Compliance & Enforcement
Failure to adhere to this IT Security Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including revocation of system access and termination of employment.

For questions regarding cybersecurity protocols or to report a security incident, contact the IT Security Team at security@company.internal.
