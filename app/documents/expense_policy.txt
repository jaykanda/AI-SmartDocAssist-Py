Company Expense Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-002

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Applies To: All Full-Time and Traveling Employees

1. Purpose & Scope
This policy establishes guidelines for incurring and reimbursing business-related expenses. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and prudence when spending company funds.

2. General Submission Guidelines
Timeframe: Employees must submit all business expense claims through the HR and finance portal within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after this window may be denied.

Receipts: Original, itemized receipts are strictly required for all reimbursement claims exceeding $25. Credit card slips alone are not sufficient documentation.

3. Eligible Expenses
Travel: Economy-class flights, standard train tickets, and reasonable ride-share or taxi services necessary for business travel require prior manager approval.

Lodging: Standard hotel accommodations booked within corporate rate guidelines. Luxury upgrades or personal incidentals (e.g., mini-bar, movies) are the employee's personal responsibility.

Meals & Entertaining: Reasonable daily meal per diems apply during business travel. Client entertainment expenses require prior approval and must list all attendees and business purposes.

4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company will not reimburse personal expenses, including but not limited to:

Traffic fines, parking tickets, or toll violations incurred during transit.

Personal grooming, gym fees, or non-business-related entertainment.

Alcoholic beverages unless part of an officially approved corporate client event.

5. Approval & Audit
Manager Approval: Manager approval is mandatory for all expense claims before finance dispatches reimbursement.

Audits: The finance department performs random compliance audits. Submitting falsified receipts or intentionally violating expense limits will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

For questions regarding expense categories, per diem limits, or international travel rates, please contact the Finance/HR Department at finance@company.internal.
